# Build a RAG chatbot over our support docs (manufacturing)

Client industry: manufacturing  
Completed: 2022-09-11

## Brief
Build a RAG chatbot over our support docs.

## Approach
Main stack: RAG, MLOps, scikit-learn. I trained a custom neural network on GPU tensors. I modelled the agent as a stateful graph with checkpoints and human approval steps.

## Outcome
The delivered system raised conversion by 52%.
