# Build a RAG chatbot over our support docs (e-commerce)

Client industry: e-commerce  
Completed: 2025-01-12

## Brief
Build a RAG chatbot over our support docs.

## Approach
Main stack: OpenAI API, scikit-learn, Python, RAG. I shipped everything as container images so staging matched production. I modelled the agent as a stateful graph with checkpoints and human approval steps.

## Outcome
The delivered system cut processing time by 48%.
