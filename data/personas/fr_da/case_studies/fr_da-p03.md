# Automate weekly Excel reporting (healthcare)

Client industry: healthcare  
Completed: 2023-11-05

## Brief
Automate weekly Excel reporting.

## Approach
Main stack: Looker, Power BI, Python, pandas. I scheduled the pipeline as DAGs with retries and alerts. I automated the spreadsheet workflow with pivot tables and macros.

## Outcome
The delivered system cut processing time by 42%.
