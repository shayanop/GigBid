# Next.js SaaS dashboard with Stripe billing (legal)

Client industry: legal  
Completed: 2024-06-16

## Brief
Next.js SaaS dashboard with Stripe billing.

## Approach
Main stack: FastAPI, Django, Redis. I used server-side rendering and file-based routing for fast first loads. I integrated subscription billing, webhooks and invoices with a payments provider.

The delivered system saved the team 19 hours a week.
