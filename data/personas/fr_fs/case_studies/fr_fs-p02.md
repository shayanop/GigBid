# Next.js SaaS dashboard with Stripe billing (logistics)

Client industry: logistics  
Completed: 2025-08-06

## Brief
Next.js SaaS dashboard with Stripe billing.

## Approach
Main stack: Next.js, Node.js. I put an in-memory cache and rate limiter in front of the database. I integrated subscription billing, webhooks and invoices with a payments provider.

## Outcome
The delivered system saved the team 28 hours a week.
