# Marketplace policy pack (GigSim, mirrors common freelance platform rules)

1. off_platform_contact: Do not share email, phone, messaging apps or social handles before a
   contract starts, and do not ask the client to.
2. off_platform_payment: All payment goes through the platform. No crypto, bank transfer or
   "pay me directly" offers.
3. fabricated_experience: Do not claim skills, clients, results or years of experience you
   cannot show. Portfolio evidence must back every claim.
4. guarantees: Do not guarantee outcomes such as rankings, revenue or "100% accuracy".
5. free_work: Do not offer or agree to unpaid sample work beyond the platform's rules.
6. automation: Proposals and messages must be reviewed and approved by the freelancer before
   sending. No mass or unreviewed submissions.
7. impersonation: Do not misrepresent who will do the work.
